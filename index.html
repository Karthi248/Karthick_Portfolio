Bus Scam Shield

Chrome extension that spots fake bus-booking sites

NextGen AI Hacks 2026 · Team Leader · Team of 4
  • Shows a green, yellow or red banner on a travel site before the user pays.
  • Scores each site on eight checks, including domain age, SSL, look-alike domains, payment gateway and operator and GST/PAN verification.
  • Combines a Random Forest model, Claude LLM analysis and Google Safe Browsing into one verdict.
  • After jury feedback, moved all API keys and checks to a Flask backend so no key is exposed in the extension.
  • Also built RSA-signed QR tickets, a scalping-risk check and a user reporting channel.

PlantCare

Crop disease detection app that works offline

Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Team Leader · Team of 6
  • A farmer photographs a leaf and gets the disease name and remedy in seconds, spoken in their regional language.
  • Trained a MobileNetV2 transfer-learning model on PlantVillage (54,000+ images) that reached 93% accuracy across 38 classes.
  • Fixed a 36x class imbalance with class-weighted loss and added rotate, zoom and flip augmentation.
  • Asks for a clearer photo when confidence is below 60% instead of giving an unreliable answer.
  • Converted the model to TensorFlow Lite so detection runs on the phone without internet.
